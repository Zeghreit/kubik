## Strategy in one line
Instrument first, then content, then reach, then a free round, then Show HN.
The paid challenge is the LAST move, gated on real numbers — not the first.

## The hooks (fixed, repeat everywhere)
1. Modelling with a thumb on a phone. Almost nobody has seen this. Flagship content.
2. No install, no account, no signup — tap a link and model.
3. One HTML file, runs offline. This is the Hacker News angle specifically.

Pitch line: "A 3D modeller that runs in your phone's browser. No install,
no account — tap the link and start modelling."

## Phases
- **W1 (7–13 Sep) Instrument & rearm** — GoatCounter analytics into index.html,
  pitch line everywhere, shoot + post the thumb-on-phone flagship, open
  YouTube/TikTok/Bluesky, build the itch.io page (HTML5 playable upload).
- **W2 (14–20 Sep) Rhythm** — daily clips, 5 genuine replies/day in other
  people's threads, the "rebuild reply" tactic, first-run pass on the app.
- **W3 (21–27 Sep) Go where they are** — open the Discord (4 channels),
  join and post in 4 other servers, pitch 80.lv.
- **W4 (28 Sep–4 Oct) Kubik Open Weekend** — free round, no prize, no judging,
  every entry reposted. Same-day bugfixes announced publicly.
- **W5 (5–11 Oct) Bank & prepare** — the recap montage (biggest post of the
  six weeks), user-confusion interviews, fix list, Show HN prep.
- **W6 (12–18 Oct) The shot** — Show HN Tue 13 Oct at 16:00–17:00 MSK, ship
  the top three complaints, then read six weeks of numbers.

## Decisions taken in discussion
- Judging by community vote is good MARKETING, weak judging. Agreed split:
  anonymised entries during voting, community vote takes People's Choice,
  Zeghreit personally awards "Best Use of Kubik" from the .json scene files.
- Requiring the .json scene with each entry doubles as anti-cheat.
- Spread the $175 across more categories than 1st/2nd/3rd; add a random draw
  among valid entries so lurkers submit.
- Reddit is off the table by preference. Discord is accepted as necessary but
  treated as a mailing list with a chat window, not a community to run.
- International payout must be solved BEFORE any prize is announced.

## Challenge gate (checked day 41, all three required)
- ≥ 8 entries in the free Open Weekend
- ≥ 50 Discord members
- ≥ 300 weekly app opens
Not met → repeat weeks 2–4 as cycle two rather than running a paid challenge.

## The one metric
Weekly unique app opens. Everything else (followers, likes) is decoration.
